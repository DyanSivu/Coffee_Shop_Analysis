# Coffee_Shop_Analysis
- Sales forecasting and exploratory analysis of multi-location coffee shop data (Jan–Jun 2023). Identifies key revenue drivers, product performance, time-based trends, and store comparisons to inform data-driven strategies for growth, optimization, and improved customer experience.

Overview
- End-to-end exploratory and sales analysis of multi-location coffee shop data, focused on extracting actionable insights to support revenue growth and operational decision-making.

Business Objective
- To identify key revenue drivers, evaluate product and store performance, and uncover opportunities to optimize sales through data-driven strategies.

Approach

- Performed data cleaning, transformation, and aggregation using Excel and Databricks
- Conducted exploratory data analysis (EDA) to assess product, time-based, and location-level performance
- Built visual dashboards to communicate trends and insights effectively
- Applied comparative and contribution analysis to evaluate revenue distribution

Tools & Technologies:
- Microsoft Excel, Databricks, Miro, Canva, Powerpoint, PowerBI

Key Insights & Impact

-Coffee and Tea contribute over 66% of total revenue, confirming core product dependency
- Bakery and Drinking Chocolate identified as high-potential categories for bundling and upselling
- Weekday sales dominate (72%), revealing strong commuter-driven demand and missed weekend opportunities
- Consistent revenue distribution across locations indicates standardized operations
- Low-performing categories flagged for optimization, repositioning, or removal

Outcome
- Delivered clear, data-backed recommendations to improve product strategy, increase revenue opportunities, and support scalable business decisions.
